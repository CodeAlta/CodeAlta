CodeAlta.Pseudonymization follows the design of Microsoft Presidio (recognizers with a score, a validation and
context words, then the resolution of overlapping results), and ports its predefined recognizers of the identifiers
every country shares (payment cards, crypto wallets, emails, IBAN with the format of each country, IP and MAC
addresses, URLs, UUIDs) and of the United States and the United Kingdom: their patterns, scores, context words and
checks (namespace CodeAlta.Pseudonymization.Presidio). The cases of the tests of these recognizers are copied in
CodeAlta.Pseudonymization.Tests/Presidio/presidio-cases.json. Ported from commit
fd78e974fbf9e15dad1f90de4fc125db39ef4200.

Phone numbers are found with libphonenumber-csharp, the .NET port of Google's libphonenumber, as Presidio finds them
with its Python port: https://github.com/twcclegg/libphonenumber-csharp, Apache License 2.0
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

The French recognizers (NIR, SIREN, SIRET, French dates and postal codes, plates, RPPS), the English ones Presidio does
not have (US employer identification numbers, UK company numbers, US ZIP codes, English dates and dates of birth) and
the checks of the NIR key (INSEE) and of the Luhn algorithm are written for CodeAlta from the public definitions of
these identifiers.
